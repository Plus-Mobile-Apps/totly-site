## Accepting the invite

1. Install Totly and sign in with the email address the invite was sent to.
2. Open **More › Notifications**.
3. Tap **Accept**, or **Decline** if you weren't expecting it.

Didn't get here from an invite email? Ask the person who invited you to send it again from
**More › Manage family**.
